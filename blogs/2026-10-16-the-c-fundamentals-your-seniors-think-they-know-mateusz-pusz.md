Pointer provenance, the One Definition Rule, name lookup, and move semantics are not beginner topics. They are the load-bearing rules that decide whether code which compiles is actually correct. This is the opening article of a series on the ones experienced C++ developers most often get wrong, each post opening on a failure that reached production.
